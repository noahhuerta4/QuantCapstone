10 Extension: three directions, two dead ends

A second round of work targeted the term the Fundamental Law identified as binding — the number of independent bets. Three directions were tried in order of how much new information each brought, rather than how promising each looked, because the Deflated Sharpe charges for search but not for evidence. Net out-of-sample Sharpe moved from 0.84 to 0.90, and the gain came from none of the three.

Direction A · rejected

Insider filings and further earnings cuts

Idea
If an earnings surprise earns its place because it has an event clock, find more event clocks. Six candidates: four built on SEC Form 4 insider filings (217,517 open-market trades, median filing lag of exactly 2 days, as the statute requires) and two further cuts of the existing XBRL panel.
Result
All six failed the inclusion gate. Insider net buying has significantly negative forecasting power in this universe — IC −0.0133 at t = −3.50.
Judgement
The sign was not flipped. It survived two artefact checks (only 1.7% of scored days are zero-filled; restricting to names that actually filed within 60 days gives −0.0145, t = −3.66), but the literature's prior is positive and there is no mechanism for a negative one. Reversing a sign because one sample says so is the exact curve-fit this project exists to detect. Revenue surprise had the best IC ever measured here (0.0175, t = 3.05) and was still rejected: at 0.46 correlation with the existing earnings sleeve it is a redundant variant, not a new bet.
Direction B · rejected at the gate

Passive execution, killed by one line of arithmetic

Idea
Hourly reversal carries roughly twenty times the effective breadth of the daily signal. It was unaffordable because crossing the spread on that much turnover costs more than the edge. But a resting limit order earns the half-spread rather than paying it — so the dominant cost might change sign rather than merely shrink.
Result
A parametric fill-quality map killed it before any microstructure work: commission + fees = 0.700 bps per dollar traded against a maximum spread capture of 0.440 bps. Even with flawless passive fills and zero adverse selection, each dollar traded still costs 0.26 bps.
Judgement
Turnover never becomes free, so the twentyfold breadth cannot be bought. The thesis rested on a cost structure this project had already disproved — once the spread was calibrated at 0.44 bps rather than assumed at 2, commission, not spread, became the dominant per-dollar cost, and earning the spread cannot flip a sign commission controls. Two hours of arithmetic replaced a multi-week simulation.
Direction C · rejected, but it found the lever

Regime conditioning, and the control that exposed it

Idea
Blend IC across quartiles of cross-sectional dispersion is monotone (−0.003, +0.008, +0.018, +0.024) and the mechanism fits — reversal is paid for providing liquidity, and both reversal sleeves strengthen with dispersion while the earnings sleeve shows no pattern at all. So scale risk with the regime.
Result
It improved the number for the wrong reason. A constant multiplier at the same average size beat the regime-varying one (0.907 against 0.878), and the diagnostic found no alpha timing whatsoever — the return gap between scaled-up and scaled-down days was −0.000004. The entire gain was reduced turnover wearing a regime costume.
Judgement
Chasing the real mechanism found the actual lever: the no-trade band, which needed re-tuning once the sleeve set had changed. It is a genuine interior optimum, and the cliff is mechanically explained — a band wider than the 1.5% per-name cap means no trade can ever clear it.

Two methodology upgrades came with this round. The walk-forward now embargoes the ten-day holding period off the end of each training window, so a position opened in training cannot still be open in the test window — without it, train and test share outcomes and the generalisation gap is flattered. And the trial count now lives in a ledger on disk, results/trial_ledger.json, under version control: every sweep with what it tried and what came of it, rejected candidates included, read automatically whenever a Sharpe is deflated.
