07 Diagnosing the result

The Fundamental Law of Active Management decomposes an information ratio into three measurable terms: IR = TC × IC × √BR, where IC is forecasting skill, BR is the number of independent bets per year, and TC is the fraction of the ideal position that actually survives constraints and costs. Used as an instrument rather than decoration, it says where a result comes from and what would change it.
