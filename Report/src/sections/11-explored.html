11 Directions explored and closed

Three substantial lines of investigation did not make it into the strategy. Each was measured rather than assumed, and the measurement is the useful part.

Intraday horizons

Breadth is the binding constraint everywhere in this project, and hourly bars are where breadth lives. On 309,573 hourly bars pulled from Interactive Brokers, hourly reversal shows an IC of 0.0095 at t = 2.9 with an autocorrelation of −0.001 — every bar a genuinely fresh bet. That is 26,059 effective bets per year against 1,309 for the daily strategy, twenty times more.

The cost of collecting it is larger than the gain. At the measured transfer coefficient the implied gross information ratio is 0.97, and break-even requires trading under 12.5% of the book per bar; even at 2% per bar the implied result does not beat what the daily strategy already delivers. The conclusion is that intraday is viable only for an operator who can execute passively — earning the spread with limit orders rather than paying it, which reverses the sign of the dominant cost term. That is a different project, and this remains a diagnostic rather than a backtest.
