04 Variables and units

Every tunable quantity in the system, with the unit it is measured in and the value used. Units are stated explicitly because most of the errors that inflate a backtest are unit errors in disguise — a spread quoted in percent instead of basis points, a rate applied per calendar day instead of per trading day.

One unit choice worth flagging. Borrow is an annual rate but the engine only steps on trading days, so it is divided by 252 rather than 360. Charging a calendar-day rate on trading days only would under-bill the short leg by about 30%. Similarly the per-order commission minimum is denominated in dollars, not basis points, which is why NAV has to be tracked in levels rather than the backtest running purely in return space.
