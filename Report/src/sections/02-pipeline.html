02 The pipeline, stage by stage

These four stages run in order, and each is independently testable. The sequence matters: the signal knows nothing about risk, the risk model knows nothing about cost, and the execution layer is the only place where money actually changes hands.

1

Signal construction

Each sleeve is computed from data through the day's close, masked to the tradable universe, clipped at ±3 median-absolute-deviations to tame outliers, then standardized cross-sectionally to mean zero and unit dispersion. Standardizing before blending is what makes the blend meaningful — raw momentum is of order 0.3 and raw two-day reversal of order 0.01, so combining the raw numbers would hand the entire book to whichever sleeve happens to have the larger units.

in: prices, volume, SEC filings → out: date × ticker score matrix
2

Risk model and position sizing

A Ledoit–Wolf shrunk covariance matrix is estimated over the tradable names on a 252-day window, refreshed monthly. Positions are sized inversely to volatility with direction and magnitude from the score, then scaled so the portfolio's ex-ante annualized volatility hits its target.

in: scores, returns → out: unconstrained target weights
3

Neutralization and constraints

Dollar, beta and sector neutrality are imposed as a single orthogonal projection rather than one after another — demeaning to get dollar neutrality would destroy beta neutrality and vice versa, so they must be solved together. Position caps and a liquidity limit are then applied, and because clipping breaks neutrality while re-projecting breaks the caps, the two alternate to a fixed point before a final uniform down-scale makes every limit hold exactly.

in: target weights → out: constrained, neutral weights
4

Execution and cost accounting

Weights formed at the day-t close are traded at the t+1 open and held to the t+2 open. Every trade pays commission, regulatory fees and slippage; every short pays borrow each day it is open. Trades below a no-trade band or below the size that clears the per-order commission minimum are skipped entirely.

in: weights, prices → out: daily P&L, trade log, cost breakdown
